## Final Answer

### HbA1c Levels

- Mild: 6.5% - 7.0%
- Moderate: 7.0% - 8.0%
- Severe: >8.0%
- Reference: ISPAD Clinical Practice Consensus Guidelines 2024.
- [Source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11854986/)

### Fasting Plasma Glucose (FPG)

- Mild: 100-125 mg/dL
- Moderate: 126-154 mg/dL
- Severe: >154 mg/dL
- Reference: IDF Clinical Practice Recommendations for Managing Type 2 Diabetes.
- [Source](https://idf.org/media/uploads/2023/05/attachments-63.pdf)

### Postprandial Plasma Glucose

- Mild: 140-199 mg/dL
- Moderate: 200-299 mg/dL
- Severe: ≥300 mg/dL
- Reference: Novo Nordisk Guideline Directed Management.
- [Source](https://www.novonordiskmedical.com/content/dam/medical/novonordiskmedical/ta/diabetes/disease-education/resource-documents/abbreviated-version-guideline-directed-management-diabetes-comorbidities.pdf)

### Body Mass Index (BMI) in Youth

- Mild: BMI ≥85th percentile
- Moderate: BMI ≥95th percentile
- Severe: BMI >99th percentile
- Reference: ISPAD Clinical Practice Consensus Guidelines 2024.
- [Source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11854986/)

### Comorbid Conditions

- Presence of hypertension, dyslipidemia, or cardiovascular disease indicates moderate to severe severity.
- Reference: IDF Clinical Practice Recommendations.
- [Source](https://idf.org/media/uploads/2023/05/attachments-63.pdf)

### Cardiorenal Risk Factors

- Moderate: Presence of either CKD (eGFR <60 mL/min/1.73 m^2) or mild heart failure.
- Severe: Established ASCVD and heart failure or severe CKD.
- Reference: Novo Nordisk Guideline Directed Management.
- [Source](https://www.novonordiskmedical.com/content/dam/medical/novonordiskmedical/ta/diabetes/disease-education/resource-documents/abbreviated-version-guideline-directed-management-diabetes-comorbidities.pdf)

### Insulin Resistance and Beta Cell Function

- Moderate: Significant insulin resistance with mild beta-cell dysfunction.
- Severe: Rapid decline in beta-cell function not responsive to treatment.
- Reference: ISPAD Clinical Practice Consensus Guidelines 2024.
- [Source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11854986/)

### Lifestyle and Social Determinants of Health (SDOH)

- Consideration of lifestyle factors like poor diet, sedentary lifestyle, and socioeconomic status can exacerbate severity.
- Reference: Novo Nordisk Guideline Directed Management.
- [Source](https://www.novonordiskmedical.com/content/dam/medical/novonordiskmedical/ta/diabetes/disease-education/resource-documents/abbreviated-version-guideline-directed-management-diabetes-comorbidities.pdf)

These criteria summarize the consensus on evaluating the severity of Type 2 Diabetes with respect to comorbidities and other factors, suitable for EHR integration.
